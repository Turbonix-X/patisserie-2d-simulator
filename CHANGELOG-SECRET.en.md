# What the changelog doesn't say

This file only shows if you know how to ask for it. It keeps what is secret in each version.

## Release 1.2.1.1

- `/changelog max` now also shows this file in the language of the changelog (English here).

## Release 1.2.1

- `/machine NAME break` (or `/machine break NAME`) breaks a machine, and `/machine break` breaks them all. `/building break` breaks all your buildings, and `/building NAME break` just one.
- Carriers created by command (`/employee create carrier …`, `/employee add`, `/max`) do not quit, even with a single bakery.

## Release 1.1.2

- `/employee hire NAME` hires at the application's price (and refuses if you don't have enough money). `/employee hire NAME free` hires for free. Only applicants are suggested.
- `/tabs customization activate` lets you arrange the tabs even before level 450, `/tabs customization deactivate` removes it, and `/tabs customization reset` restores the original order.
- `/max`, `/employee add` and `/employee create` no longer create carriers with a single bakery.

## Release 1.1.1

- Suggestions work like in Minecraft: type `/ingredient`, every ingredient shows up, click one, then add a space to see what you can do next.
- `/max` now sets all employees to level 10.
- `/employee fire NAME` fires an employee, `/employee hire NAME` hires an applicant for free, and `/employee reject NAME` refuses them.
- `/employee create JOB NAME LEVEL` creates an employee (level from 1 to 11). For a collector: `/employee create collector NAME LEVEL INGREDIENT LOSS`.
- Bob's gold no longer goes away with 10 clicks: drag milk onto him and he turns purple again.

## Release 1.1.0

- All commands are now in English and singular. An ingredient changes with `/ingredient strawberry set 4`, a recipe with `/recipe cookie set 3`, and `/buy flour 10` or `/machine set oven 3` also work with English names.
- `/employee NAME skill set N` changes an employee's skill, and `/employee NAME level set N` their level (from 1 to 11).
- `/pause` stops time, but you can still play with your ingredients and you lose nothing. `/pause off` resumes.
- `/theme see NAME` shows you a theme just once, without unlocking it. `/theme see off` goes back to your theme.
- `/changelog max` adds this file to the changelog, and `/changelog unmax` removes it.
- The Bakery theme (level 100) and the Cookie theme (1 million cookies sold) are hidden until you find them.
- When Bob is gold, 10 clicks on him make the gold go away.
- A level 11 exists: it only comes through applications, from level 120. It is 10 times better than level 10, very rare, and the only one with a gold badge.
- The jack of all trades comes from two-job searches: someone who has both can apply.
- A collector of level 6 or more can be specialized in an ingredient: their training is ten times faster.

## Before 1.1

- 1000 clicks on Bob without the console unlock a secret theme.
- Feed Bob wasabi and he chokes. With 5 gold leaves, he turns gold, and a gold theme appears.
